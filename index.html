Steroids Given?
Route

Prednisolone 1mg/kg (round up to nearest 5mg): <2yr max 10mg OD | 2-5yr max 20mg OD | >5yr max 30mg OD | ≥12yr max 40mg OD. Hydrocortisone IV: 4mg/kg 6-hrly (max 100mg/dose) or by age — 1mo-1yr 25mg, 2-4yr 50mg, 5-18yr 100mg, all 6-hrly.

Given?
Route

Age 2-17yr: 40mg/kg single dose over 20 min (max 2g). Use 50% injection diluted to 10% with 4x volume NaCl 0.9%. Requires cardiac monitoring + BP every 5 min during infusion. If not responding within 15 min → discuss with on-call paediatric consultant, consider second-line IV.

Second-Line Given?
Agent

IV Salbutamol bolus over 5 min: <2yr 5mcg/kg (max 250mcg), ≥2yr 15mcg/kg (max 250mcg).
IV Aminophylline loading dose 5mg/kg (max 500mg) — do NOT give if already on theophylline. Maintenance infusion: 1mo-11yr 1mg/kg/hr; 12-17yr 500-700mcg/kg/hr.
